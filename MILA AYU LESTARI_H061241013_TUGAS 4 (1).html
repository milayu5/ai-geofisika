        Data notes
        • Source: Ultimate_Sales_Profit_Dataset.xlsx (sheet “Data”), 500 orders, embedded in this file; every number is computed in your browser.
        • Partial years: 2023 contains May–Dec only and 2025 contains Jan–Apr only, so year totals are not comparable. Trend arrows compare matched calendar windows.
        • Returns: Sales and Profit include orders flagged as returned, exactly as recorded in the source. Net-of-returns revenue may be lower.
        • Profit: Gross Profit and Net Profit are identical to Profit in every row, so only Profit is shown. Overall margin = total profit ÷ total sales (not an average of order margins).
